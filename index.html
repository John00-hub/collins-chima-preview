How a brief runs

Three steps, no mystery.

STEP ONE

The first meeting

You bring the facts and whatever papers you have. We tell you what the law allows, what it does not, and roughly what it will cost.

STEP TWO

The written terms

Scope, timeline and fees go on paper before any filing, so you sign knowing what you are paying for and when each stage falls due.

STEP THREE

The file stays open

One counsel carries the matter from start to finish and reports at every stage, so you are never the last person to hear about your own case.
